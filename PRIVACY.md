# DoseTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23.11.2026.

## Summary

DoseTrack does not collect, transmit or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The app has no internet access, no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party services. The developer never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

This policy applies to the DoseTrack app for Android (package `com.dosetrack`), published by Suncan Stilinovic.

## Data stored on your device

DoseTrack only stores what you type into it, in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

| Data | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Medications | Name, dose amount and unit |
| Injection log | Date and time, injection site on the body diagram, medication used |
| Reactions and notes | Whether you had a reaction, and any notes you write |
| Settings | Reminder schedule, site history length, display preferences |

This is health information, so treat your phone's screen lock as the main protection for it. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any device identifiers.

## Permissions

DoseTrack asks for three permissions, all used only for injection reminders. It does not request internet access.

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | To show your injection reminder |
| Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) | To deliver the reminder at the exact time you chose |
| Run at startup | To set your reminder again after the phone restarts or the time zone changes |

You can turn reminders off, or revoke any of these permissions in Android settings. The rest of the app keeps working without them.

## Backups and exports

Your data leaves the device only when you choose to export it, and every export is encrypted with a password you set.

- **Backup files.** When you create a backup, DoseTrack encrypts it with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your password. You decide where it goes: a folder you pick, or an app you choose in the Android share menu. Whatever happens to the file after that is governed by that app or service, not by DoseTrack.
- **Your password.** The password is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the backup cannot be opened, and the developer cannot recover it.
- **Importing.** Restoring a backup reads the file you select and adds its contents to the app's local storage.
- **Android cloud backup.** DoseTrack opts out of Google's automatic cloud backup, so your data is never uploaded to your Google account.
- **Phone-to-phone transfer.** When you set up a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy DoseTrack's data to the new phone. That transfer goes straight between your devices.

## Sharing with third parties

DoseTrack shares no data with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking libraries, and it never sells data.

Google Play may collect information about app installs and crashes under [Google's own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). DoseTrack has no access to the content of your log.

## Your control

You can delete any injection entry or medication inside the app at any time. To erase everything, uninstall DoseTrack, or use **Settings > Apps > DoseTrack > Storage > Clear storage**. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side.

**Children.** DoseTrack is meant for adults managing their own treatment. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

**Not medical advice.** DoseTrack is a personal logging tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or recommend doses. Always follow the guidance of your doctor or pharmacist.
